# Holiday PTO Calendar

A month-at-a-time calendar for your paid time off. This copy starts empty. Your days stay in your own browser.

## Open it

https://bonnieann-k.github.io/holiday-calendar-template/

Bookmark that link. You do not need to install anything.

## First-time setup

1. Click **Setup**
2. Enter your name, starting hours, and monthly accrual
3. Click **Import PTO** and drop in the Excel file from Workday **My Time Off**
4. Click **Save setup**

To get that Workday file: sign in to Workday, search **My Time Off**, then download Excel from the upper right.
